Rinse & Roam

The spa pulls up to your curb. Mobile dog grooming for San Jose.

The problem

  • Salon grooming means drop-off windows, car rides and hours in a kennel.
  • Anxious, senior and large dogs suffer most.
  • Busy owners lose half a day to a bath.

The solution

A climate-controlled grooming van that parks outside the customer's home. One dog at a time, with its own water and power. Bath, blow dry, trim and nails, then the dog walks back inside.

Who it's for

Busy households
Want their time back.
Anxious, senior and large dogs
Do better without kennels.
Rescues and daycares
Need reliable grooming partners.

How it works

  • Owner tells us about the dog and picks a curbside window.
  • We text when the van is minutes away.
  • Groom takes place in the van; payment is taken at the curb or online.

Market

The U.S. pet industry reached about $158B in 2025, projected $165B in 2026 (APPA). Several mobile groomers already serve San Jose, so we compete on experience and reliability. Benchmarks: $75 average (HomeGuide) up to $120 to $350+ (Barkbus).

Sources: research.md [1], [4], [5]. A grooming-only market size was not found.
